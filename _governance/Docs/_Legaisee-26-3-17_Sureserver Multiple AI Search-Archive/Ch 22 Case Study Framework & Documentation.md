Chapter 22: Case Study Framework & Documentation
22.1 The Proof Imperative
One documented case study beats 10 pitch decks. When you have zero clients, you have zero credibility. Your first client's primary value is not the $2,000 — it's the case study they become.
The Documentation Discipline:
Capture everything (you cannot document retroactively)
Specificity creates credibility ("40% increase" beats "great results")
Before/after contrast sells transformation
Every client becomes a referral engine (if you engineer it)

22.2 The 5-Point Case Study Architecture
Every case study follows this narrative structure:
Table
Point
Content
Purpose
1. The Before
Problem, pain, failed attempts
Creates identification ("that's us")
2. The Excavation
Assets found, golden era, abandonment diagnosis
Demonstrates your unique methodology
3. The Transformation
Modernization, system build, team adoption
Shows implementation capability
4. The After
Metrics, quotes, timeline to results
Provides proof
5. The Continuation
Retainer status, future plans, referral
Suggests ongoing value
