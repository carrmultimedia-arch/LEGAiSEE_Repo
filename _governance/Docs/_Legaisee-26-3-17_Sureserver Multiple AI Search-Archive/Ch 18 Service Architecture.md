The Process (5 Days):

Day 1: Deep Excavation
├── Multi-AI queries across 40+ channels
├── Wayback Machine website archaeology  
├── Social media timeline reconstruction
└── Competitive landscape mapping

Day 2: Client Interview
├── 60-minute discovery call
├── Old employee/story gathering prompts
├── Email campaign history request
└── Internal document collection

Day 3: Synthesis & Analysis
├── Pattern recognition across eras
├── Psychological trigger identification
├── Abandonment causation analysis
└── Competitive gap assessment

Day 4: Report Drafting
├── 10-page structure completion
├── Asset curation and tagging
└── Video walkthrough recording

├── 60-minute strategy call
├── Stage 2 offer presentation
└── Proposal for full system build

Offer money-back guarantee if no valuable assets found (rarely claimed)
