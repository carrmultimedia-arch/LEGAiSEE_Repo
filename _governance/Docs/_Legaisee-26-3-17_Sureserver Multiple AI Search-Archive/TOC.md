The Verification System (Your Insight)
When sources conflict (previous owner says X, employees say Y, records show Z):
Source Quality Hierarchy
Tier 1: Contemporaneous documents, financial records, third-party verification
Tier 2: Single witness with specifics, physical artifacts
Tier 3: Vague recollections, self-interested testimony
Tier 4: Hearsay, suspected falsehoods
The 3-Source Rule
Minimum 2 independent sources for any claim
Ideally 3+ converging sources
Or 1 Tier 1 source alone is sufficient
Uncertainty Grading
Every finding labeled:
[VERIFIED] — Multiple Tier 1, no contradiction
[PROBABLE] — Tier 1 + Tier 2 support, minor gaps
[PLAUSIBLE] — Tier 2 only, no direct contradiction
[UNCERTAIN] — Single source or unresolved conflict
[UNVERIFIED] — Claim made, no supporting evidence
