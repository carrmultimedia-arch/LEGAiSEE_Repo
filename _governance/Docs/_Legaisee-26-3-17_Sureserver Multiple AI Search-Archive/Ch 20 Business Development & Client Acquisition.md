20.3 Zero-Network Prospecting (Manual Outreach)
For businesses that don't find you, you find them.
The Research Protocol (30 minutes per prospect):
plainCopy
STEP 1: YOUTUBE ARCHAEOLOGY (10 min)
Search: "[city] [industry] commercial [year]"
Example: "Houston car dealership commercial 1990s"
Look for: Old TV spots, abandoned channels, production quality
Document: URLs, dates, what made them good

STEP 2: WAYBACK MACHINE COMPARISON (5 min)
Compare: 2017 vs 2024 homepage
Look for: Removed services, changed messaging, design degradation
Document: Screenshots, specific changes

STEP 3: LINKEDIN GHOST EMPLOYEE TRACKING (10 min)
Search: "[Company] marketing director" 
Look for: Former employees who moved on
Message: "I noticed you used to run marketing at [Company]. 
I'm researching their history. Would you share what was 
working when you were there?"

STEP 4: GOOGLE AWARD SEARCH (5 min)
Search: "Addy Award [city] [industry]"
Look for: Past professional investment, quality work
Document: Award years, categories, what they won for
Target Volume: 3 personalized outreaches per day
Time Investment: 90 minutes daily
Weekly Output: 15 prospects researched, 3-5 contacted

20.5 Discovery Call Architecture (30 Minutes)
The goal: Extract their story, demonstrate pattern recognition, transition to audit offer.
Phase 1: Opening (0-2 minutes)
plainCopy
SCRIPT:
"Thanks for taking the call. Before we dive in — are you the 
person who makes marketing decisions for [Company], or should 
I also include [partner/CEO/marketing director]?"

[If not decision-maker]: "Who else should be involved if we 
move forward?"

[If decision-maker]: "Good. I have 30 minutes blocked. Here's 
how I'd like to use it: I'll share what I found in my research, 
then I'd love to hear the history from your perspective. Sound 
good?"
Phase 2: The Story (2-12 minutes)
plainCopy
SCRIPT:
"From my research, it looks like [Company] had a strong run 
in [era] with [specific tactic]. Then something shifted around 
[year]. Tell me about that era — what was working then?"

LISTEN FOR:
- Golden era details (what, when, who, why)
- Transition triggers (what changed, why stopped)
- Current frustrations (what's not working now)
- Nostalgia signals ("those were the days")

PROBING QUESTIONS:
- "Who was running marketing then?"
- "What did customers say about you?"
- "Why did that campaign stop?"
- "What replaced it? Did it work?"
- "If you could bring back one thing, what?"
Phase 3: The Diagnosis (12-20 minutes)
plainCopy
SCRIPT:
"I'm hearing a pattern. [Summarize: Golden era → Abandonment → 
Current struggle]. This is common — businesses abandon working 
tactics before their time, often chasing trends that don't fit.

The good news: [specific tactic] worked because of [psychology], 
not because of [era-specific technology]. That psychology still 
works — it just needs modern execution.

What I do is excavate those proven assets, modernize them for 
today's platforms, and build systems to deploy them repeatedly.

For [Company], I'd want to do a full Business History Audit. 
That means:
- Deep excavation across 40+ channels
- 15-25 specific assets documented
- Analysis of what made them work
- Modernization recommendations
- 10-page report + video walkthrough

The investment is $2,000, delivered in 5 business days.

Does that make sense for where [Company] is right now?"
Phase 4: The Transition (20-26 minutes)
plainCopy
IF INTERESTED:
"Great. I can start Monday or Wednesday. Which works better? 
I'll send the proposal and invoice now — payment locks your 
spot. Any questions before I send it?"

IF HESITANT:
"What questions do you need answered?"

IF RESISTANT:
"I understand — it's an unusual approach. Take 48 hours. 
I'll follow up Wednesday with one specific asset I think 
could work for you. Fair?"
Phase 5: Close or Follow-Up (26-30 minutes)
plainCopy
IF CLOSED:
- Confirm start date
- Send proposal + invoice before end of call
- Schedule kickoff call
- Update CRM: "Closed — Audit, $2,000, starts [date]"

IF FOLLOW-UP:
- Confirm specific follow-up date/time
- Note their specific hesitation in CRM
- Prepare targeted asset for follow-up
- Send thank-you email with snapshot attached

20.6 Objection Handling Scripts
Table
Objection
Response
"I need to think about it"
"What specific questions do you need answered? I can address them now, or I can send you a reference from a similar client. Would that help?"
"That's too expensive"
"I get it. Let me ask — what's the cost of not knowing what worked? If [golden era tactic] brought in [estimate] customers, and you've been without it for [years]... what's that worth?"
"Can you do it for less?"
"I don't discount, but I do apply audit credits toward the full system build. If you move to Stage 2 after the audit, I'll credit the full $2,000. Does that work?"
"I need to check with my partner"
"Absolutely. When's the best time to include them? I'd rather answer their questions directly than play telephone. Tuesday or Thursday?"
"We've tried marketing consultants before"
"Most consultants start with 'what should you do?' I start with 'what did you do that worked?' Different approach. If you want another generic strategy, I'm not your guy. If you want to excavate proven assets, I am."
"What guarantee do we have?"
"The audit guarantees clarity — you'll know exactly what worked, when, and why. Results depend on execution, which is why I offer the system build. But the diagnosis itself is guaranteed. If I find nothing, you don't pay."
