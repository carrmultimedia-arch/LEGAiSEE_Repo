21.6 Pain-Managed Daily Schedule
Your body is the business constraint. Respect it.
plainCopy
8:00-10:00: DEEP WORK BLOCK
- Seated at ergonomic setup
- Heating pad on
- 10-minute break at 9:00
- Tasks: Report writing, AI workflow building, analysis

10:00-10:30: FULL REST
- Reclined position
- Ice or heat (alternate)
- No screens
- Possible: Voice-to-text prep for later

10:30-12:30: CLIENT CALL BLOCK
- Headset for hands-free
- 10-minute break at 11:30
- Tasks: Discovery calls, delivery calls, async video recording

12:30-2:00: LUNCH + REST
- Reclined if possible
- Light meal
- Possible 20-minute nap

2:00-4:00: LIGHT WORK BLOCK
- Seated or standing (alternate)
- 10-minute break at 3:00
- Tasks: Email, prospecting research, scheduling, admin

4:00: HARD STOP
- No exceptions
- Begin recovery mode
- Evening: Rest, family, hobbies, sleep preparation
Total: 5.5 hours active work + 1 hour breaks = 6.5 hours committed
Weekly: 32 hours maximum (6.5 × 5 days)

21.7 High-Pain-Day Protocol
Recognition:
Pain level 6/10 or higher
Sleep failed (less than 4 hours)
Medication ineffective
Cannot sit comfortably for 30+ minutes
Action:
plainCopy
IMMEDIATE:
- Cancel or reschedule all client calls
- Send: "Medical issue today, rescheduling available [specific times]"
- Reduce to 3 hours maximum work

WORK ALLOWED (async only):
- Voice-to-text drafting (Google Docs, Apple Dictation)
- Light email responses (1-2 sentences)
- Template updates
- No calls, no meetings, no deep analysis

RECOVERY:
- Day 1-3: Full rest (reclined, ice/heat, no work guilt)
- Day 4-7: Gradual return (2 hrs → 4 hrs → 6 hrs → full)

COMMUNICATION:
- Be direct: "Managing a health flare-up"
- No apology needed: "Back Thursday at full capacity"
- Protect boundaries: Do not accept "urgent" requests
