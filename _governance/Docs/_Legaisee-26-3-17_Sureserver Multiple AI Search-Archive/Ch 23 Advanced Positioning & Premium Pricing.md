23.1 The Luxury Imperative
You cannot outwork a broken body. Your 32-hour weekly limit is not a constraint to overcome — it's a positioning advantage. Luxury clients don't want volume. They want your full attention on their legacy.
The Scarcity Principle:
8 clients maximum (Foundation phase)
5 clients maximum (Elevation phase)
3 clients maximum (Premium phase)
The Psychology: When you say "I serve 3 clients per year," you signal:
Exclusivity (they must qualify for access)
Depth (you're not spread thin)
Status (association with you transfers prestige)
