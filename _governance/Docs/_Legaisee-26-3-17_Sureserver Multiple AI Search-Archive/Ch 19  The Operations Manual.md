Chapter 19: The Operations Manual
19.1 Execution Philosophy
Theory without execution is worthless. This chapter provides click-by-click workflows for delivering each service stage. Follow these exactly until you have enough experience to improvise.
Core Principles:
Table
Principle
Application
Systematize everything
If you do it twice, document it
Batch similar tasks
All client calls same day, all writing another day
Async over sync
Video messages beat meetings; docs beat calls
48-hour response max
Never let clients wait, never respond instantly (sets boundaries)
No weekends
Protect recovery time (critical with your health constraints)


19.2 Stage 1: The Audit — 5-Day Execution
Total Time Budget: 20 hours over 5 days
Daily Maximum: 4 hours (your pain-managed schedule)

Day 1: Multi-AI Excavation (4 hours)
Morning (2 hours): Platform Queries
Your Archaeology Intelligence Platform executes 40+ channel searches. For each client, run these queries across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity:
plainCopy
QUERY SET A: Historical Advertising
- "[Company Name] commercial 1990s"
- "[Company Name] old advertisement"
- "[Company Name] vintage marketing"
- "[Company Name] TV spot archive"

QUERY SET B: Digital Footprint
- "site:[companydomain.com] 2010..2015"
- "[Company Name] Wayback Machine"
- "[Company Name] old website"
- "[Company Name] discontinued products"

QUERY SET C: Competitive Context
- "[Industry] [city] marketing 2000s"
- "[Competitor] vs [Company Name] history"
- "[Industry] golden era advertising"

QUERY SET D: Cultural Markers
- "[Company Name] Addy Award"
- "[Company Name] best of [city]"
- "[Founder Name] [Company Name] interview"
Capture Protocol:
Save each AI response to your platform (auto-captured via extension)
Tag by: channel, era, asset type, quality score (1-5)
Screenshot visual assets immediately (links rot)
Note: Source URL, date found, confidence level
Afternoon (2 hours): Manual Deep Dive
Wayback Machine: 5-10 historical homepage snapshots
YouTube: Search "[Company] commercial", filter by upload date (oldest first)
Facebook Ad Library: Current competitor ads (reverse-engineer their strategy)
LinkedIn: Company page history, founder profile, employee count over time
Google News: Archive search for press mentions, awards, controversies
Day 1 Deliverable: Raw asset collection (30-50 items), preliminary timeline notes

Day 2: Client Interview (4 hours)
Pre-Call (30 min):
Review Day 1 findings
Prepare 3 "golden era" hypotheses to test
Set up recording (Otter.ai or Zoom)
The 60-Minute Interview Structure:
plainCopy
MINUTE 0-5: Opening & Context
"Based on my initial research, I found [specific asset from 1998]. 
Tell me about that era — what was working then?"

MINUTE 5-20: The Golden Era
- When did you feel the business was firing on all cylinders?
- What marketing were you doing then?
- Who was involved? What agencies? What internal team?
- What did customers say about you then?

MINUTE 20-35: The Transition
- When did things shift? What happened?
- New competition? Leadership change? Market disruption?
- What marketing got cut first? Why?
- What replaced it? Did it work?

MINUTE 35-50: Current State
- What marketing are you doing now?
- What's working? What's not?
- What do customers say now vs. then?
- If you could bring back one thing from the past, what?

MINUTE 50-60: Asset Collection Request
"I'd like to dig through your archives. Can you send me:
- Old email campaigns
- Marketing materials from [golden era years]
- Photos of old ads, brochures, signage
- Any stories from longtime employees"

**End with:** "I'll deliver your audit Friday. We'll find 15-25 assets minimum."
Post-Call (2.5 hours):
Transcribe recording (Otter.ai auto-transcribes)
Tag quotes by theme: golden era, transition, current pain, nostalgia
Update asset inventory with client-provided materials
Refine timeline hypothesis
Day 2 Deliverable: Interview transcript, tagged quotes, updated asset list, confirmed timeline

Day 3: Synthesis & Analysis (4 hours)
Morning (2 hours): Pattern Recognition
Review all collected assets. Identify:
Table
Pattern Type
What to Look For

The 5-Point Analysis Framework:
For each major era, document:
The Asset: Specific campaign, dates, production quality evidence
The Abandonment: When stopped, what replaced it, why
The Current State: Neutral description of present marketing
The Competitive Gap: Who occupied the space they vacated
The Question: Research inquiry inviting deeper investigation
Afternoon (2 hours): Competitive Context
Map 3-5 competitors who entered/thrived as client declined
Document competitor's current tactics (screenshot, archive)
Identify "abandoned territory" — spaces client used to own
Calculate opportunity cost (rough estimate acceptable)
Day 3 Deliverable: Pattern analysis doc, 5-point frameworks, competitive map, opportunity sizing

Day 4: Report Production (4 hours)
The 10-Page Report Structure:
plainCopy
PAGE 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- One-sentence golden era identification
- Three bullet points: what worked, when lost, cost of loss
- One recommendation preview

PAGES 2-3: HISTORICAL ANALYSIS
- Timeline: Rise → Peak → Decline
- Key assets from each phase (3-5 examples with images)
- What the market looked like then vs. now

PAGES 4-5: ASSET INVENTORY
- 15-25 excavated assets table
- Columns: Asset, Era, Channel, Quality, Status (abandoned/adapted/active)

PAGES 6-7: ABANDONMENT DIAGNOSIS
- Why each major tactic stopped (data, politics, trend-chasing, budget)
- What replaced it (and whether it worked)
- Cumulative effect on market position

PAGE 8: COMPETITIVE CONTEXT
- Who took their place
- What tactics competitors use now
- "Abandoned territory" map

PAGE 9: RESTORATION ASSESSMENT
- Which assets are revivable (nostalgia, timeless principles)
- Which are era-locked (technology-dependent, culturally dated)
- Modernization difficulty rating (Easy/Medium/Hard)

PAGE 10: STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION
- Top 3 assets to modernize
- Suggested channels for revival
- Next step: Authority Systems Build proposal
Video Walkthrough (30 min Loom):
Screen share the report
Talk through key findings conversationally
Show excitement about specific discoveries
End with: "Ready to build the system? Let's talk."
Day 4 Deliverable: Complete 10-page report, Loom video link, Stage 2 proposal draft

Day 5: Delivery Call (4 hours)
Pre-Call (30 min):
Review report one final time
Prepare Stage 2 proposal ($5,500 midpoint)
Anticipate objections
The 60-Minute Delivery Call:
plainCopy
MINUTE 0-10: Report Walkthrough
Share screen, flip through report, let them react to findings

MINUTE 10-30: Deep Dive on Key Assets
Focus on 2-3 assets that clearly resonate
"What would it look like to bring this back for 2026?"

MINUTE 30-45: The Diagnosis
Present abandonment analysis
"These weren't bad tactics. They were abandoned before their time."

MINUTE 45-55: The Transition to Stage 2
"I can modernize these assets and build you a system to produce 
authority content continuously. The investment is $5,500, and 
here's what you get..." [present proposal]

MINUTE 55-60: Close or Schedule Follow-Up
If ready: Schedule kickoff call
If hesitant: "What questions do you need answered?"
If resistant: "Take 48 hours. I'll follow up Wednesday."
Post-Call (2 hours):
Send proposal via email (PDF + Loom summary)
Update CRM (Notion/Airtable) with status
Schedule follow-up if no immediate close
Begin case study documentation
Day 5 Deliverable: Completed delivery, proposal sent, next steps scheduled

Days 9-10: AI Workflow Construction (8 hours)
Build the actual AI-powered production system:
plainCopy
WORKFLOW 1: Script Generation
Input: Topic + pillar + platform
AI Prompt: "Write a [platform] script about [topic] in the style of [client voice]. 
Hook in 3 seconds. Include pattern interrupt at 0:30. CTA at end."
Output: 3 variant scripts
Human: Select + light edit

WORKFLOW 2: Hook Testing  
Input: 5 script openings
AI Prompt: "Rate these hooks for curiosity gap, specificity, and emotional trigger. 
Suggest improvements."
Output: Ranked list with suggestions
Human: Select top 2

WORKFLOW 3: Repurposing Engine
Input: Long-form content (video, article, podcast)
AI Prompt: "Extract 5 short clips, 3 quote graphics, 1 email summary, 
2 tweet threads from this content."
Output: Asset package
Human: Review + schedule

WORKFLOW 4: Title/Thumbnail Optimization
Input: Content topic
AI Prompt: "Generate 10 titles using: curiosity gap, specificity, 
emotional trigger, how-to, listicle formats. Rate for CTR potential."
Output: Ranked titles + thumbnail concepts
Human: Select + design
Document each workflow with:
Exact prompts used
Expected output format
Quality thresholds
Human intervention points
Week 2 Deliverable: Content pillar system, 4 core AI workflows, prompt library
