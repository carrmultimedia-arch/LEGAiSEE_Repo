Most consultants cannot do what you can.
Your background combines:

AI tools
Most business consultants understand operations but can’t uncover historical assets.
Most marketers understand branding but can’t reconstruct company history.
Most AI consultants understand prompts but don’t understand narrative or institutional memory.
LEGAiSEE sits at the intersection of all three.
The first clients will probably not buy because of the “AI” or even because of “Business Archaeology.”
They’ll buy when you uncover something valuable they forgot they had.
That suggests a practical first-client strategy:
Find companies with history.
Perform a small excavation before asking for money.
Show them something surprising.
Offer a deeper paid excavation.
That approach usually converts better than cold-pitching a completely new service category that people have never heard of.
