Beta-Gemstone-Sprint
Manual first clients
This is the "Beta-Gemstone" Sprint. We are going to bypass the "Million Dollar Giants" for one week to land a $5,000–$7,000 "proof of concept" client in New Braunfels. This pays your bills, stress-tests your server, and gives you the case study for the $25k corridor titans.

Day 1: The Reconnaissance (8:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
Goal: Identify 3 local "Legacy" businesses with visible institutional amnesia.
Open Google Maps: Search "Construction," "HVAC," "Law Firms," or "Roofing" in New Braunfels.
The Filter: Look for names that sound like people, not keywords (e.g., "Lowry Electrical" vs. "Best New Braunfels Electricians").
The Website Click: Go to their site. If it looks like a generic 2018 WordPress template with stock photos of people shaking hands, they are your target.
The Shortlist: Select one (e.g., a 30-year-old local HVAC company).
The "Who": Use LinkedIn to find the Owner or President. Do not talk to a Marketing Manager.
Day 2: The Shallow Dig (9:00 AM – 3:00 PM)
Goal: Find the "Ghost of Marketing Past."
Wayback Machine: Paste their URL. Go back to 2004–2008.
The Click: Look for the "About Us" or "Our Story" page from 20 years ago. Find a specific photo of the original founder, an old truck with the original logo, or a slogan that isn't on their site today (e.g., "Our Family Keeping Yours Cool Since '82").
YouTube Search: Search "[Company Name] New Braunfels" and sort by Upload Date: Oldest.
The Artifact: Find a 15-year-old local cable commercial or a grainy "training video."
The "Breakthrough" Logic: If you find a slogan they used for 5+ years but abandoned in 2015 for something generic like "Quality You Can Trust," you’ve found your gemstone.
Day 3: The Alchemistry (10:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
Goal: Create the "Audit of Authority" Pitch Deck.
Upscale: Take that grainy 2005 photo or the old logo. Run it through your Topaz Photo AI or your local AI server upscaler. Turn it into a 4K "Heritage" asset.
The Comparison Slide: Create a "Side-by-Side" in Canva or PowerPoint:
Left Side: Current Website (Template/Generic/Weak).
Right Side: The "LEGAISEE Vision" (The upscaled heritage photo, a black/gold/obsidian color palette, and their original 1990s slogan).
The "Authority Systems" Slide: List your 3 phases: Archeology (Finding the equity), Alchemistry (Modernizing the assets), Prestige Authority (The $25k/mo long-term maintenance).
The Price: Note the "Beta Phase" price of $5,000 (One-time Audit + 1 Heritage Video) with a $2,500/mo maintenance retainer thereafter.
Day 4: The Sovereign Outreach (11:00 AM)
Goal: Secure the meeting without being "spammy."
The Physical Drop-Off: Print your "Comparison Slide" on high-quality cardstock. Put it in a black envelope.
The Delivery: Drive to their office in New Braunfels. Walk in and ask for the Owner by name.
The Script: "I’m John. I’ve been in broadcast production for 35 years. I’m doing a Business Archeology study on New Braunfels' founding companies. I found a $2M asset your company abandoned in 2008. I’ve already upscaled it and want to show you the revenue potential of bringing it back. I have 10 minutes on Tuesday at 2:00 PM."
The Exit: Hand them the envelope. Leave immediately. Do not "sell."
Day 5: The Meeting & The Close (2:00 PM)
Goal: Move them from "Institutional Amnesia" to "Sovereign Authority."
The Frame: You are not a "Marketing Guy." You are a Forensic Auditor.
The Reveal: Show the pitch deck. Show the upscaled 1990s photo.
The Pivot: "Right now, you look like a startup with a template website. You are competing on price. This 1990s slogan/story is why people actually trust you. My system polishes this history so you can stop chasing leads and start commanding a premium."
The Close (The Exclusivity Play): "I am only taking one HVAC company [or their industry] in the New Braunfels area for this Beta rollout. I wanted to start with you because of your 40-year history. If you'd like the 'Heritage' assets I've already started, the audit fee is $5,000. We can start the deep-dive Monday."
The Payday (Immediate)
The Invoice: Use Stripe or PayPal. Send the link while sitting in the office or via text as you walk to your car.
The Onboarding: Give them a simple "Vault Access" form (requesting old hard drives, photos, and tapes).
The Result: You have $5,000 in your account and a client who is now terrified of their competitor getting your "Archeology" services.

Why this works:
You are selling Certainty. A business owner who has spent 35 years building a company is terrified of being "forgotten." When you show up with a polished piece of their own history, you aren't selling a service—you are returning a lost family heirloom.
