For a product like Legaisee’s Business Archaeology & Authority Systems, I’d price it as a premium consulting/strategy offer, not a commodity service. The right charge is likely in the $7,500–$25,000+ range depending on depth, deliverables, and whether you’re selling a diagnostic, a roadmap, or implementation support.
A practical pricing model
A strong starting structure would be:
Diagnostic / audit only: $3,500–$7,500.
Authority system buildout: $10,000–$18,000.
Full archaeology + authority system + advisory support: $20,000–$35,000+.
Ongoing retainer: $1,500–$5,000/month for updates, monitoring, and support.
That pricing fits the kind of work you describe on the site: mapping what already exists across many channels, then turning that into a usable system rather than a simple one-off deliverable.
Why that range fits
The offer appears to be highly specialized, research-heavy, and tailored to the client’s existing business assets and records, which supports premium pricing over generic consulting. Similar professional service work in archaeology and compliance-adjacent advisory spaces is commonly priced as a custom engagement rather than fixed low-ticket packages, and professional guides emphasize understanding scope, risks, and budget before commissioning the work. In adjacent professional-services markets, publicly posted pricing often starts in the hundreds or low thousands for narrow services, but your offer is broader and more strategic, so it should sit well above that.
Best way to package it
I’d suggest three tiers:
Foundation: $4,500.
Business archaeology scan.
Key findings summary.
30-day action list.
Growth: $12,500.
Deeper mapping across channels and records.
Authority system architecture.
Messaging and positioning recommendations.
Authority: $24,000.
Full scan, synthesis, system design, rollout plan.
2–3 implementation sessions.
30–60 days of support.
That gives buyers a clear choice while anchoring the premium tier as the main offer.
What to decide before finalizing price
Your price should move up if you include any of these:
Direct implementation done-for-you.
Confidential interviews or internal record reconstruction.
Industry-specific expertise.
Executive-level strategy sessions.
Ongoing support or monitoring.
Your price should move down only if the deliverable is mostly self-serve, lightly customized, or very short in duration.
My recommendation
If this is a new offer and you want to validate demand, launch with $9,500–$12,500 as your core package and a premium tier at $19,500–$25,000. If you already have proof, testimonials, or clear business outcomes, you can start higher.
