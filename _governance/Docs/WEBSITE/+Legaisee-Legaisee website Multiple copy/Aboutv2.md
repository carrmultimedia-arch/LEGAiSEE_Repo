About / Methodology — Final Narrative Copy
At Legaisee, we practice Business Archaeology—a disciplined excavation of your company’s history, culture, and communications. This is not marketing guesswork or digital surface analysis. Every insight emerges from a structured research protocol designed to reconstruct your business story in its entirety, from the 1890s to the present, across eight major classes and 40+ specific channels.
We examine every available signal: print, broadcast, digital, internal documents, and human testimony. Each signal is layered by era, contextualized, and weighed for credibility. We do not simply “look at your past”; we operate a classified research protocol across media history, extracting patterns, contradictions, and evolutions invisible to conventional marketing audits.
Our methodology rests on ten interlocking frameworks:
The 40+ Channel Taxonomy — Every channel is examined with temporal precision. From early print ads to modern social media, each source is cataloged by decade and type, ensuring methodical coverage, not guesswork. This scale demonstrates both discipline and authority, signaling that we do what no agency does: reconstruct a business history across time, media, and cultural shifts.
Source Hierarchy (Tier 1–4) — Not all information is created equal. Tier 1 sources are verified; Tier 2 probable; Tier 3 plausible; Tier 4 uncertain. Every claim is graded. This hierarchy communicates procedural rigor and ensures that conclusions are not assumptions but evidence-based reconstructions.
Triangulation Protocol — When sources conflict, we document, assess, and synthesize. Contradictory data—whether from budget lines, archived ads, or recollections—is not ignored or cherry-picked. Resolving conflicts transparently builds trust and separates us from agencies that default to narrative convenience.
Three-Source Rule — No significant claim is accepted without two independent sources or one Tier 1 source. This is non-negotiable. Constraints like this are credibility multipliers, signaling that we have rules we refuse to break.
Private Archaeology Protocol — Our research reaches beyond the internet. Digital artifacts, physical archives, and human testimony are systematically accessed, including data points unavailable to ordinary marketers. This operational depth positions us closer to investigators, archivists, and intelligence analysts than to conventional content agencies.
Continuous Verification Model — Findings are never static. Addendums, revised reports, and dispute resolutions keep the work living and accurate. Accuracy, not ego, drives our output.
Eight-Dimension Voice Profile — We model your company’s communication across eight linguistic dimensions: vocabulary, sentence architecture, humor, perspective, transitions, calls-to-action, evidence style, and visual language. This ensures that all outputs speak authentically in your voice while reinforcing intellectual authority.
Why It Works Framework — Our methodology leverages the Lindy Effect, pattern recognition, institutional memory loss, and competitive asymmetry. This intellectual backbone converts insights into unavoidable logic, not just interesting anecdotes.
Ideal Conditions / ROI Triggers — We identify where our work yields maximum impact: pre-2010 marketing investments, founder transitions, multi-generational businesses, and decline after peak. This clarity qualifies leads instantly, making the right client feel seen and understood.
Channel Time Span (1890s → Present) — Historical depth is subtle but crucial. This is not a snapshot; it is a reconstruction across media evolution, reframing our work as investigative and historical rather than purely digital.
Every project is executed with dual-layered precision: a narrative, insider-level voice for emotional resonance, and a procedural, evidence-based layer for credibility. The result is a living map of your company’s trajectory, strengths, vulnerabilities, and opportunities, grounded in both intellectual rigor and storytelling mastery.
