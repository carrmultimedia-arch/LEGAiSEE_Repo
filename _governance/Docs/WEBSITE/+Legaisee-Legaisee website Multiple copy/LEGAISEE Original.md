METHODOLOGY PAGE
The Legaisee Method: Documentary Rigor Applied to Business
Introduction:
Business Archaeology applies the standards of historical research to marketing strategy. We don't speculate. We excavate, triangulate, and verify.

￼
The 8-Dimension Voice Profile
Before we write a word of content, we capture your authentic communication style across eight dimensions:
1. Vocabulary Register — Technical vs. accessible, jargon density, formality
2. Sentence Architecture — Length, complexity, rhythm, fragment usage
3. Humor & Tone — Type, frequency, enthusiasm, emotional openness
4. Perspective & Stance — Expert vs. peer, teacher vs. learner, optimist vs. realist
5. Transitions & Flow — Connector preferences, storytelling signals, pacing
6. CTAs & Directives — Command strength, urgency, benefit framing
7. Evidence & Proof — Data usage, anecdote style, authority signaling
8. Visual Language — Metaphor frequency, sensory detail, scene-setting
Result: AI systems that write in your voice, not generic "marketing voice."

￼
Source Quality Hierarchy
Tier 1 (Verified):
 • Contemporaneous documents with metadata
 • Financial records (budgets, invoices, actual spend)
 • Third-party verification (industry publications, awards, news coverage)
 • Multiple independent witnesses with matching stories
Tier 2 (Probable):
 • Single witness with specific details (dates, names, amounts)
 • Physical artifacts with provenance
 • Partial documents corroborated by other sources
Tier 3 (Plausible):
 • Single witness with vague recollections
 • Memory without documentation
 • Self-interested testimony
Tier 4 (Uncertain):
 • Hearsay
 • Suspected falsehoods
 • Agenda-driven claims without corroboration
Every finding in our reports carries a confidence label. We don't hide uncertainty—we document it.

￼
The Triangulation Protocol
When sources conflict (and they do), we:
1. Document the conflict — All versions recorded
2. Assess credibility — Tier rating, bias analysis, motive examination
3. Seek resolution — "How could both be partially true?"
4. Synthesize transparently — "Source A claims X, Source B claims Y. The most probable synthesis is Z, with [confidence level]."
Example:
"Previous owner denies TV advertising; two employees confirm 'Game Day Special' spots; budget document shows $60K 'Broadcast' line item 2000. Conclusion: Significant TV presence 2000-2001, likely discontinued 2002. Previous owner may confuse with later period. [Probable]"

￼
The 3-Source Rule
No significant claim appears in our reports without:
 • Minimum 2 independent sources, OR
 • 1 Tier 1 source alone
This is non-negotiable. We'd rather report "uncertain" than be wrong.

￼
The Private Archaeology Protocol
Public sources find the surface. We go deeper:
Digital Artifacts: Old hard drives, email archives, CRM exports, cloud accounts, design files, video projects
Physical Artifacts: Filing cabinets, print samples, direct mail, trade show materials, awards, correspondence
Human Artifacts: Previous owners, longtime employees, customers, vendors, industry historians
Access Negotiation: We tailor our approach to positive, neutral, or negative relationships with gatekeepers. We respect refusal. We triangulate around gaps.

￼
Continuous Verification
Our reports are living documents. New information emerges:
 • Addendums: Minor corrections or additions (free)
 • Revised Reports: Major new findings (50% of audit price)
 • Dispute Resolution: If sources challenge findings, we investigate and correct if warranted
We never defend errors. We correct them.

ADDITIONAL PAGE: FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
How is Business Archaeology different from market research?
Market research tells you what customers want now. Business Archaeology tells you what made customers choose you then—and how to make them choose you again. We study your history, not just your market.
What if we don't have archives or old files?
We triangulate from multiple sources. Employee interviews, customer memories, public records, competitor observations—we build the picture from whatever fragments exist. Some of our richest excavations came from businesses that thought they had "nothing."
How long does it take to see results?
Stage 1 (Audit) delivers clarity in 5-7 days. Stage 2 (Systems) takes 21 days to build, then 30-90 days to see metric movement. Stage 3 (Partnership) optimizes continuously. Most clients see engagement improvements in 30 days, lead improvements in 60-90 days.
Do we have to use AI?
Our systems use AI to scale your voice, not replace it. You approve everything. The AI generates drafts; you refine. Over time, the AI learns, and your effort decreases. But you're always in control.
What if we don't like what you find?
Sometimes archaeology reveals uncomfortable truths (bad decisions, missed opportunities). We deliver findings with context and forward-looking recommendations. The past informs; it doesn't condemn.
Can you work with our existing marketing team?
Yes. We often enhance rather than replace. Your team gets systems that make them more effective, not directives that ignore their expertise.
What industries don't fit Business Archaeology?
Startups without history, businesses that never invested in marketing, companies seeking "viral" shortcuts, or organizations where we can't access decision-makers.
How do you handle confidential information?
NDAs standard. Source confidentiality respected (we anonymize when requested). Private documents returned or destroyed per client preference. We treat your history with the discretion of archivists.
