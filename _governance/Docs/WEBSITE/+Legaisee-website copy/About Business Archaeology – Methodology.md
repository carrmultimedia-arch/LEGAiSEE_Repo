Operations & Infrastructure – Physical assets, locations, supply chain decisions.
Digital Presence – Websites, social media, digital archives, SEO history.

Personnel & Leadership – Founders, executives, staff decisions, key hires.
Customer & Market Data – Surveys, reviews, retention, public sentiment.
Competitive Landscape – Competitor tracking, market shifts, industry patterns.
Each channel is assigned a tiered credibility score:
Tier 1 – Verified: Documented, confirmed, primary sources
Tier 2 – Probable: Highly reliable secondary sources
Tier 3 – Plausible: Contextual evidence, reasonable assumption
Tier 4 – Uncertain: Unverified claims, minor relevance
We do not allow significant claims without either 2 independent sources or 1 Tier 1 source. This is non-negotiable.

Triangulation Protocol
When sources conflict, we document, assess, and synthesize:
Record each source verbatim
Compare against other data points
Resolve discrepancies using weighted credibility
Capture unresolved conflicts transparently
This ensures your business’s history is accurate, contextualized, and actionable.
