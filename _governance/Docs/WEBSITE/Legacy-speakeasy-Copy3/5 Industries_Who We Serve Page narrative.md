WHO WE SERVE — SPEAKEASY VERSION

Not every business is a good fit for this.
That’s not positioning.
It’s just how the process works.

This only works when there’s something to go back to.
Time.
 Activity.
 Decisions that played out over more than just a few months.
If everything is brand new, there’s nothing to excavate.

But most businesses aren’t new.
They’ve been operating long enough to have history—
 they just don’t think of it that way.

A retail business that’s run campaigns for years
 but never connected them into anything consistent.
A restaurant that once had strong customer engagement
 that slowly faded without a clear reason.
A real estate group that’s sitting on years of listings, deals, and market movement
 but isn’t using any of it strategically.
A professional service firm that’s had hundreds—sometimes thousands—of client interactions
 without ever stepping back to see the patterns inside them.

Different industries.
Same situation.

What matters isn’t the category.
It’s the depth.

If there’s enough there—enough content, enough decisions, enough movement—
 patterns start to show up.
And once they do, they can be followed.
Reconstructed.
Used.

That’s why most engagements tend to fall into a similar range.
Businesses that have been active for at least a few years.
 That have created, tested, published, adjusted.
Not perfectly.
Just consistently enough that something exists beneath the surface.

The methodology doesn’t change from one industry to another.
The context does.
Where we look.
 What we prioritize.
 How things are interpreted.
But the process itself stays the same.
Survey.
 Excavation.
 Restoration.
 Preservation.
 Exhibition.

Some industries make this more obvious.
Others hide it better.
But the underlying structure is almost always there.

What usually determines fit isn’t size.
It’s whether there’s something real to uncover—
 and whether you’re willing to look at it without assumptions.

If you’re unsure, that’s normal.
Most people don’t know what’s there until they see it laid out.

That’s what the initial survey is for.
Not to commit to anything.
Just to find out if this applies to you.
