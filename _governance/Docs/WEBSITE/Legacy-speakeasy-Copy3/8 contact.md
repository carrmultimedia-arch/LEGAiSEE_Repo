CONTACT — SPEAKEASY / HIGH-TRUST VERSION

Most people don’t reach out right away.
They read.
 They think about it.
 They recognize pieces of what’s being described.
And then at some point, it clicks—
there might actually be something there worth looking at.

This is where that shifts from internal… to external.
Not a long process.
Just enough to establish context.

When you reach out, you’re not filling out a form for the sake of it.
You’re giving me a starting point.
A name tells me where to look.
 A business gives me scope.
 A few notes—if you choose to include them—usually say more than you think.
Most people already know where something feels off.
They just haven’t traced it yet.

Nothing you send disappears into a system.
It comes directly to me.
I review it personally.
Not to qualify you in the usual sense—
 but to understand whether there’s something worth examining,
 and where that might begin.

From there, the next step is simple.
If there’s signal, I’ll outline what I’m seeing
 and where I would start.
If there isn’t, I’ll tell you that too.
No ambiguity.

You won’t get a generic response.
And you won’t get pushed into anything.
This only works if the starting point is real.

Timing is usually short.
Most initial responses happen within a couple of days,
 depending on current excavation workload.
I keep that intentionally limited.
Too many active engagements, and the work loses accuracy.

If we move forward, it begins lightly.
A survey.
Nothing invasive.
Just enough to see what’s actually there.

That’s all this is.
An entry point.

Most people hesitate here.
Not because it’s complicated—
but because they suspect what they might find.

If you’re ready to look, send it through.
