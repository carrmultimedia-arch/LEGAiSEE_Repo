Real Archaeology	Legaizee Business Archaeology

Phase 2: Site Survey & Reconnaissance
Real Archaeology	Legaizee Business Archaeology
Walkover survey — Archaeologists physically walk the site, note surface features	Digital walkover — Browse current website, social media, review sites; note what's present vs. what's missing
Geophysical survey — Ground-penetrating radar (GPR), magnetometry, electrical resistivity to detect buried features	Geophysical survey — SEO analysis (what keywords they used to rank for), backlink archaeology (who linked to them in 2010), traffic pattern reconstruction via SimilarWeb/Ahrefs historical data
Trial trenches — Small test digs to confirm deposits exist before full excavation	Shallow Dig lead magnet — 15-minute automated excavation: business name + website → 1-page snapshot of what AI finds
Environmental assessment — Soil composition, drainage, preservation conditions	Market environment scan — Current competitive landscape, industry trends, customer sentiment analysis
Stakeholder consultation — Landowners, local communities, indigenous groups	Stakeholder mapping — Identify current owner, previous owner, longtime employees, former marketing directors

Phase 3: Excavation Planning & Permits
Real Archaeology	Legaizee Business Archaeology
Research design — Define research questions, excavation strategy, methodology	Audit scope definition — What eras to investigate? What channels? What depth? (5 years? 15 years? Founding?)

Phase 4: The Dig / Excavation
Real Archaeology	Legaizee Business Archaeology
Stratigraphic excavation — Remove soil layer by layer, oldest at bottom	Chronological excavation — Work backwards from present: 2024 → 2020 → 2015 → 2010 → founding era
Context recording — Every find documented: which layer, which grid square, spatial relationships	Context recording — Every asset tagged: era, channel, asset type, source quality tier, confidence level
Feature identification — Distinguish pits, walls, hearths, burials from natural deposits	Feature identification — Distinguish campaigns from ongoing tactics, identify "golden era" vs. decline periods
Artifact recovery — Tools: trowels, brushes, dental picks for delicate items	Artifact recovery — Tools: Wayback Machine, YouTube downloads, archive.org, old hard drive imaging, email PST extraction
In situ photography — Photograph artifacts exactly where found before moving	In situ capture — Screenshot original context: surrounding webpage, publication date, comments, engagement metrics
Soil sampling — Collect samples for flotation, pollen analysis, C-14 dating	Data sampling — Collect performance metrics, budget documents, A/B test results, customer feedback from era
Daily site diary — Record weather, progress, decisions, anomalies	Daily excavation log — Record queries run, sources found, dead ends, breakthroughs, hypothesis shifts

Phase 6: Artifact Processing & Analysis
Real Archaeology	Legaizee Business Archaeology
Cleaning — Remove soil with distilled water, soft brushes, chemical treatments	Cleaning — Restore degraded images, transcribe old videos, convert obsolete file formats, OCR scanned documents
Conservation — Stabilize fragile materials: adhesives, consolidants, controlled environment	Conservation — Digital preservation: backup to multiple formats, metadata embedding, version control
Cataloging — Unique ID, description, dimensions, material, date, provenance	Cataloging — Asset ID, era, channel, asset type, source tier, confidence level, file path, local mirror path
Typological analysis — Classify by form, function, style, cultural affiliation	Typological analysis — Classify by 8 primary classes, 40+ subclasses, era tags, transformation status
Scientific analysis — C-14 dating, thermoluminescence, metallurgy, DNA	Scientific analysis — Performance attribution (what results did this asset drive?), competitive benchmarking, ROI reconstruction
Comparative study — Compare with similar finds from other sites	Comparative study — Compare with competitor campaigns from same era, industry benchmarks, cross-client pattern recognition
Triangulation — Cross-reference multiple sources to verify authenticity	Triangulation — Reconcile conflicting sources: previous owner says X, employee says Y, budget document says Z
Uncertainty grading — Label confidence: verified / probable / plausible / uncertain / unverified	Uncertainty grading — Same system applied to every finding in the audit report
